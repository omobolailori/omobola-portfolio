Acorn is a biotech platform that lets individuals bank their own stem cells for future regenerative use. By harvesting hair-follicle cells and cryopreserving them at ultra-low temperatures, it enables long-term access to pristine cells for skin, hair, injury, or disease therapy. I redesigned the website and designed the mobile app that helps patients track their cell storage and treatment progress.
